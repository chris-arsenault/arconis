# Backlog

Planned-but-not-built work. Each item is a positive assertion of future-state behavior.

## Agents

- Let a person ask an assistant from the canvas, with one key on a selected topic sending
  "expand this" with the branch as context, the result shown as pending branches to accept or
  discard, once an assistant can be reached from the browser rather than only through MCP.
- Keep lines routed around items from crossing or overlapping one another where they can run apart.
- Let agents add pictures.
- Let a person grant an assistant read-only access to a room.
- Let connectors register through client metadata documents as well as dynamic client registration,
  once a released Auth0 Terraform provider sets dynamic registration by metadata document.

## Canvas

- Crop pictures in place, as the one geometry edit pictures need beyond resizing.
- Let a connector carry a label at each end.
- Add a minimap, hidden until asked for.
- Insert space by dragging empty canvas, pushing everything beyond the drag apart.
- Add a focus mode that hides the chrome with a persistent way back.
- Let a person disable single-key shortcuts for assistive technology, and give pictures and icons
  alternative text that agents also read.
- Add a brace over several sibling topics and a boundary that grows with its subtree, and level
  styles that map depth to colour and size and survive reparenting.
- Re-route attached connectors as objects move, so a routed line stays clear of obstacles without
  rerunning **Route around items**.
- Keep manual placements when a tree or flow is tidied again, so one edit moves only what it must.
- Design a table item whose rows and columns grow and resize together.
- Let a person choose the canvas background colour, including a dark canvas.
- Give a topic a long note that folds away beneath its label.
- Let a person mark stencils and icons as favourites and find them first.
- Let a person rebind keyboard shortcuts.
- Offer starter canvases on the welcome screen, supplied as canvas-language files.

## Interchange

- Read PlantUML activity diagrams as native items.
- Carry icons and notes across when importing a diagram.

## Sheet view

- Show the canvas's items as a table whose columns are their tags, marker, task, properties, layer
  and last editor, with sorting and in-place editing of those fields.
- Group the same rows into a board by tag, marker or task state, where moving a card changes
  that field.
- Keep the sheet a view of canvas items, as Outline is of trees: choosing a row flies to its
  object, and the Find and filter chips narrow both.

## Plans

- Leave a measurement on the drawing as a dimension line that follows the points it measures and
  reads in the display unit.
- Show the area of a closed path in the drawing's units.

## Drawing

- Let a stylus's eraser end and barrel button erase while held, returning to the tool in hand.
- Straighten a stroke held still at its end, and offer shape recognition, each as a setting a
  person can leave off.

## Slides

- Export a deck as PDF or PPTX, one page per slide.
- Let participants follow the presenter's slide, and show notes on a second screen that the
  audience never sees.
- Animate the move between slides, with a setting that reduces motion.
- Reveal a tree level by level while presenting from it.
- Wrap the selection in a slide that adopts it.

- Investigate Foundry VTT's live drawing and presence tools, such as a long press to ping a spot
  for everyone, and bring over the ones that suit a shared canvas.
